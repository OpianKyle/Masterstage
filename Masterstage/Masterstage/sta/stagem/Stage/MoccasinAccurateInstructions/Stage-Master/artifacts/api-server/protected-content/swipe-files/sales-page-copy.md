## 4. The NLP Edge section

**Eyebrow**
The unfair advantage

**Headline**
Some people say something ordinary and the room leans in. It was never charisma.

**Intro**
It's 12 patterns. They have names. They can be learned in an afternoon and they work whether
you're confident or terrified — because they live in the words and the sound, not in your
personality. Every speaking course on the internet skips them. This one installs one pair per
module.

**The 12 patterns (card grid)**

1. **Reframe** — Kill the nerves in one sentence by renaming what you're feeling.
2. **Confidence Anchor** — A physical trigger that drops you into your best state on command.
3. **Presupposition** — Bake the yes into the grammar. Say "when", never "if".
4. **Embedded Commands** — Plant the instruction inside an ordinary sentence. They never see it.
5. **Analog Marking** — Make one line unforgettable with sound alone. Slower. Lower. Silence.
6. **Auditory Pacing** — Meet their energy, then drag it upward. Never open at your peak.
7. **State Transfer** — Whatever you feel, they catch. Decide it before you hit record.
8. **Nested Loops** — Open a story, hold the ending hostage, and they cannot leave.
9. **Submodality Shifts** — Direct the movie in their head. Before: dark and tight. After: bright and close.
10. **Pace-and-Lead** — Three truths they already believe — then the pivot they can't argue with.
11. **Future Pacing** — Let them own the result before you ever say the price.
12. **The Swish** — Overwrite the old self-image. Identity first, behaviour follows.

**The ethical rule (keep this — it's a differentiator, not a disclaimer)**
One rule, and it's non-negotiable: every pattern here makes TRUE things land harder. Used on
a claim you can't back up, it just gets you caught faster.
